# StockPro Homepage Competitor and Pattern Matrix

Research date: 2026-07-16  
Scope: public homepage/product documentation research for an original StockPro marketing concept. No wording, layout, visual identity, assets, or interaction cadence should be copied.

## Method and limitation

The review used current public HTML/search-indexed content from official sites. The requested in-app Browser connection was unavailable, so visual details that require live interaction, computed accessibility trees, or performance traces are recorded as **QA risks**, not as measured failures. This matrix includes 18 relevant fintech, market-research, SaaS, WebGL, and accessible-visualization references.

## Cross-reference conclusions

1. **The task must appear before the spectacle.** Screener.in, Wise, Stripe, Coinbase, and Groww all make the first meaningful action legible immediately. StockPro should render headline, disclosure, search/readiness, and primary CTA before loading Three.js.
2. **Trust is a state, not a visual mood.** Dhan and Wise place qualifying information close to the action. StockPro must bind provider, timestamp, broker, and account states to real HTML; glow, green color, or motion cannot imply readiness.
3. **A product ecosystem needs a narrative spine.** Zerodha, Upstox, Linear, and Vercel succeed by grouping features around user intent and sequence. StockPro’s ten scenes should tell one story: discover → verify → understand → save → connect safely → begin.
4. **One world is preferable to ten effects.** The Bruno Simon reference supports a coherent renderer, but its game navigation is the wrong interaction model for finance. StockPro should keep native scroll and use the world only as an explanatory substrate.
5. **The accessible alternative is the product, not a backup.** Highcharts accessibility guidance and the market-tool references support a complete semantic path. The HTML version must remain usable when WebGL, animation, live data, or provider access is absent.
6. **Originality comes from StockPro’s evidence model.** The visual language should be derived from research primitives—range, filter, verification, boundary, saved state—not from another brand’s palette, layout, or assets.

## Reference principle-to-transformation ledger

| Learned principle | StockPro transformation | Non-copying constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Search-first utility | Provider-aware stock research entry in the hero | Do not reuse Screener.in’s layout, wording, examples, or visual styling. |
| Numbered product narrative | Ten evidence-bearing StockPro scenes | Do not mimic Linear’s proportions, typography, footage, or pacing. |
| Transparent status near action | Provider/timestamp/readiness text adjacent to affected CTAs | Do not reuse Dhan/Wise disclosures or claim language. |
| Cohesive WebGL world | One procedural research universe with named scene states | Do not reuse Bruno Simon’s driving metaphor, camera, assets, or controls. |
| Accessible chart contract | HTML descriptions, routes, status, and structured fallback | Do not imply a canvas becomes accessible through ARIA alone. |
